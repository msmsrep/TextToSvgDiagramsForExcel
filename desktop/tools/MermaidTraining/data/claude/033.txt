=== flowchart
A task starts in To Do. When someone picks it up it moves to In Progress. From there it moves to Review when the work is finished. A reviewer either moves it to Done or sends it back to In Progress.
---
flowchart TD
    A["To Do"] -->|"Someone picks it up"| B["In Progress"]
    B -->|"Work is finished"| C["Review"]
    C -->|"Approved"| D["Done"]
    C -->|"Sent back"| B

The air conditioner has three modes. In Cool mode it runs the compressor until the room reaches the target. In Fan mode it only moves air. In Auto mode it switches between Cool and Fan depending on the temperature. The remote cycles through the modes: Cool, Fan, Auto, and back to Cool.
---
flowchart TD
    A["Cool"] -->|"Remote"| B["Fan"]
    B -->|"Remote"| C["Auto"]
    C -->|"Remote"| A
